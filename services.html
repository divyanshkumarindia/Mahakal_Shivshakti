Our Specialized Services

Integrated solutions built to ensure continuous, high-grade fuel supply for thermal power stations, steel foundries, cement kilns, and industrial plants across India.

COMPREHENSIVE SOLUTIONS

End-to-End Coal Logistics & Supply Chain

SERVICE 01

1. Sourcing & Procurement

Leveraging our extensive network across major Coal India Limited subsidiaries (SECL, WCL, MCL, NCL), we secure consistent coal allocations under FSA (Fuel Supply Agreements) and Spot / Exclusive E-Auctions.

  • Coal India E-Auction & Spot Procurement
  • GCV, Moisture & Ash Lab Testing
  • Imported Coal Sourcing (Indonesian & South African)
  • Pithead Rake Allotment Facilitation
SERVICE 02

2. Stockyard & Handling Operations

Our strategic stockyards near major railway sidings enable bulk storage, screening, and rapid mechanical loading, protecting your inventory from transit loss and weather degradation.

  • Mechanical Conveyor & Payloader Loading
  • Size Crushing & Screening Facilities
  • Dust Suppression & Temperature Monitoring
  • 24/7 Security & Weighbridge Calibration
SERVICE 03

3. Multimodal Road & Rail Logistics

Combining Indian Railways BOXN/BOBR rake transport with our dedicated fleet of multi-axle dump trucks, we guarantee rapid turnaround times and minimal demurrage.

  • Dedicated Indian Railways Rake Coordination
  • 100+ Multi-Axle Dump Trucks Fleet
  • GPS Fleet Tracking & Transit Supervision
  • Zero-Demurrage Loading Guarantees
SERVICE 04

4. Inventory & Stock Management

We offer automated inventory analytics and scheduled replenishment pipelines to maintain optimal safety stock levels at your manufacturing or power facility.

  • Consumption Rate & Re-Order Planning
  • Live Stockyard Inventory Audit Reports
  • Customized Buffer Stock Reserves
  • Cost Optimization & Transit Shrinkage Minimization
